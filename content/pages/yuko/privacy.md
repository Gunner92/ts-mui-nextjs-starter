      This policy describes how BG Mobile Dev ("we", "us", or "our") handles
      information in **Yuko Scan for Food Safety** for iPhone and iPad (bundle
      identifier `burak.fatih.right.food`, the "App"). It is specific to Yuko
      and, for this App, it replaces our
      [general privacy policy](/privacy-policy).


      ## The short version


      - There is **no account**. No sign-up, no login, no e-mail address, no
      password — none exists anywhere in the App.

      - We run **no user database**. No server of ours holds your scans,
      photos, health profiles or history.

      - Your health profiles, allergen and condition settings, scan history,
      saved products and achievement badges live **on your device**.

      - Label reading happens **on your device**: the text on an ingredient
      label is recognised by Apple's on-device Vision framework, and the
      additive explanations are generated by Apple's on-device language model
      where the device supports it. Photos of labels are not uploaded to us or
      to any AI provider.

      - When you scan a **barcode**, the barcode number is sent to the
      Open Food Facts database to fetch the product's ingredient list. The
      request carries no identity.

      - The App uses **Firebase** for crash reports, anonymous usage analytics
      and remote configuration. It shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency
      prompt, but it does not use the advertising identifier to track you
      across other companies' apps or websites, and it shows no advertising.


      ## No account, no user database


      Yuko has no registration of any kind. We never ask for your name, e-mail
      address, phone number, postal address or a password, and we have no way
      to associate the App's data with your identity. Because there is no
      account, there is also no server-side profile to breach, sell or hand
      over.


      ## What stays on your device


      The following is created and kept locally in the App's own storage on
      your device:


      - Your health profiles: the names, icons, allergens and health
      conditions you enter for yourself and your family members

      - Your scan history and saved products, including the verdicts shown for
      them

      - Your achievement badges and learning progress

      - Your settings, notification preferences, whether you have completed
      onboarding, and the household choices you made during onboarding


      We do not receive a copy of any of it. You can delete profiles, history
      entries and saved products inside the App, and deleting the App removes
      all of this local data.


      ## How scanning works


      **Photo and camera scans.** When you point the camera at an ingredient
      label or pick a photo, the text is recognised by Apple's Vision
      framework on your device and matched against the App's built-in additive
      database, which is also stored on your device. The photo never leaves
      your device, and we never see it.


      **Additive explanations.** On devices that support Apple Intelligence,
      the App may use Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework to phrase
      an explanation of an additive. Processing happens on the device; nothing
      is sent to us or to a third-party AI provider.


      **Barcode scans.** When you scan a product barcode, the App sends the
      barcode number to the **Open Food Facts** API
      (world.openfoodfacts.org) to fetch that product's ingredient list and
      Nutri-Score. The request contains the barcode number and the App's
      standard network headers; it carries no name, no account and no
      identifier of yours. Open Food Facts is a non-profit, open database
      governed by its own
      [terms of use](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use). Product
      data shown from it is licensed under the Open Database License.


      ## Health news


      If you keep the optional health-news card or notification on, the App
      fetches public food-safety feeds directly from the publishing
      authorities (for example the European Food Safety Authority and the U.S.
      Food and Drug Administration). These are plain feed requests to those
      organisations' websites and carry no identity. You can turn the card and
      the notification off in Settings.


      ## Notifications


      All notifications Yuko shows — badge celebrations, scan reminders and
      health-news alerts — are scheduled locally on your device, and each type
      can be switched off in Settings. The App registers for push
      notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging so that we could send
      service announcements in the future; we do not currently send any, and
      the registration token is not linked to anything that identifies you.


      ## Analytics, crash reports and configuration


      The App includes Google's **Firebase** SDK, which provides:


      - **Crashlytics** — crash and performance reports, so we can fix
      problems. These contain the device model, OS version and the state of
      the App at the time of a crash. They are not linked to your identity.

      - **Analytics** — anonymous usage events such as which screens are
      opened and whether a scan completed, together with a random
      app-instance identifier and device information. We use this to
      understand how the App is used and to improve it. We do not use it to
      build a profile of you across other apps or websites.

      - **Remote Config** — switches that let us adjust the App's layout and
      copy without a new release. This involves no personal data.


      The App asks for permission through Apple's **App Tracking
      Transparency** prompt. Whether you allow or deny it, the App does not
      track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not share data
      with data brokers and shows no advertising; the prompt exists so that
      the analytics SDK's behaviour is covered by your explicit choice.


      ## Purchases and subscriptions


      Purchases are processed entirely by **Apple's App Store**. We never see
      or receive your payment card, billing address or Apple Account
      credentials. The App reads your entitlement locally from StoreKit on your
      device to decide whether your subscription is active.


      Apple also sends us **App Store Server Notifications** about subscription
      events for this App — a purchase, a renewal, a cancellation, a refund.
      Those messages identify a transaction, not a person: they carry no name,
      no e-mail address and no postal address. We do not keep them beyond
      short-lived server logs.


      Subscriptions are governed by Apple's
      [standard Terms of Use (EULA)](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).
      You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings;
      deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.


      ## Refund requests


      If you ask Apple for a refund of a Yuko purchase, Apple may ask us for
      information about your use of the app. In that case we may share with
      Apple how much you used Yuko (for example how many labels you scanned),
      whether the purchase included a free trial, and how long you have used
      the app, so that Apple can make a fair decision. Apple makes the refund
      decision, not us. We do not share your scans, photos or any health
      profile.


      ## Service providers


      We share data only with the service providers needed to run the App,
      and only for that purpose:


      - **Google Firebase** (Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config, Cloud
      Messaging), under Google's
      [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

      - **Open Food Facts**, which receives barcode numbers for product
      lookups.

      - **Netlify**, which hosts the endpoint that receives Apple's
      subscription notifications.


      We do not sell data, and we do not share it with anyone else.


      ## How long data is kept


      - **Everything on your device:** until you delete it in the App or
      delete the App.

      - **Crash reports and analytics events:** kept by Firebase under
      Google's standard retention, which we have not extended.

      - **Subscription notifications:** not kept beyond short-lived server
      logs.


      ## Camera and photo library


      The App asks for camera access so you can scan labels and barcodes, and
      lets you pick label photos from your photo library. Photos are processed
      on your device only. You can change these permissions at any time in
      iOS Settings.


      ## Health information


      The allergens and health conditions you enter are used solely to decide,
      on your device, which additives to warn you about. They are never
      uploaded, and the App makes no medical diagnosis. The warnings are
      informational and are based on the public regulatory sources cited
      inside the App.


      ## Your rights


      Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct,
      delete or port personal information, or to object to its processing.
      Because we hold no user database and no account, the practical answer to
      almost every such request is the delete controls in the App and deleting
      the App itself. If you believe we hold something about you, write to us
      at the address below and we will respond.


      ## Children


      The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
      collect personal information from children. Because there is no account,
      we have no children's records to hold.


      ## Security


      The design is the security measure: no account to compromise, no server
      of ours holding your scans, photos or health profiles, and label reading
      that never leaves the device. Traffic between the App and the services
      above is encrypted in transit.


      ## Changes to this policy


      We may update this policy as the App changes. The "last updated" date at
      the top of this page always reflects the current version, and material
      changes will be reflected in the App's disclosures in the same release.
