      This policy describes how BG Mobile Dev ("we", "us", or "our") handles
      information in **Level Tool** for iPhone and iPad (bundle identifier
      `tr.com.bgmobiledev.leveltool`, the "App"). It is specific to Level Tool,
      it is effective from October 18, 2026, and, for this App, it replaces
      our [general privacy policy](/privacy-policy).


      ## The short version


      - There is **no account**. No sign-up, no login, no e-mail address, no
      password — none exists anywhere in the App.

      - The App **makes no network requests of its own**. Every measurement is
      calculated on your device, and nothing you measure is sent to us or to
      anyone else.

      - Measurements are shown live and are not saved. The few things the App
      remembers — your settings and onboarding answers — stay **on your
      device**.

      - The camera is used only for live AR measuring. No photo or video is
      recorded, saved or uploaded.

      - Location permission is used only so that iOS can point the compass to
      true north. The App never reads your location coordinates.

      - There is **no analytics SDK, no advertising and no tracking**, and the
      App never shows an App Tracking Transparency prompt.


      ## No account, no user database


      Level Tool has no registration of any kind. We never ask for your name,
      e-mail address, phone number, postal address or a password. We run no
      server that receives data from the App, so there is no user database and
      no profile of you anywhere on our side.


      ## What stays on your device


      The App keeps only the following, locally in its own storage on your
      device:


      - Your settings, such as the unit system you measure in

      - Your onboarding answers (your goals, how often you take on DIY
      projects, the tools you already own and your preferred units) and
      whether you have completed onboarding

      - A few values the App uses to decide when to ask for a rating


      We do not receive a copy of any of it, and deleting the App removes it.


      ## How the tools work


      **Level, protractor, stud finder and metal detector.** These read the
      device's motion sensors: the accelerometer for the level and protractor,
      and the magnetometer for the stud finder and metal detector. The
      readings are processed in real time on your device and are not stored.


      **AR ruler and height measure.** These use Apple's ARKit with the
      camera. The camera image is analysed live on your device to place points
      in the space around you; no photo or video is recorded, saved or
      uploaded.


      **Compass.** The compass uses the heading that iOS provides. iOS asks
      for location access ("While Using the App") so that it can correct
      magnetic north to true north. The App receives only the heading — it does
      not request or read your location coordinates, and nothing is stored or
      sent. If you decline, the compass shows magnetic north.


      ## Analytics and advertising


      Level Tool contains **no analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDK**
      and **no advertising**. It does not use the advertising identifier
      (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites. The only
      third-party code in the App is an animation library (Lottie) that plays
      animations bundled with the App. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to
      share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us crash reports and
      aggregated usage statistics, which do not identify you.


      ## Purchases and subscriptions


      Purchases are processed entirely by **Apple's App Store**. We never see
      or receive your payment card, billing address or Apple Account
      credentials. The App reads your entitlement locally from StoreKit on your
      device to decide whether your subscription is active.


      Apple also sends us **App Store Server Notifications** about subscription
      events for this App — a purchase, a renewal, a cancellation, a refund.
      Those messages identify a transaction, not a person: they carry no name,
      no e-mail address and no postal address. We do not keep them beyond
      short-lived server logs.


      Subscriptions are governed by Apple's
      [standard Terms of Use (EULA)](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).
      You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings;
      deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.


      ## Refund requests


      If you ask Apple for a refund, Apple may ask us for information about
      your use of the App. For Level Tool **we do not answer these requests**:
      no information about your use of the App is sent to Apple, and Apple
      makes the refund decision on its own.


      ## Service providers


      The App itself shares nothing with third parties. On our side:


      - **Apple** processes purchases and subscriptions and sends us the
      subscription notifications described above.

      - **Netlify** hosts the endpoint that receives those notifications.


      We do not sell data, and we do not share it with anyone else.


      ## How long data is kept


      - **Everything on your device:** until you delete the App.

      - **Subscription notifications:** not kept beyond short-lived server
      logs.


      ## Camera, location and motion sensors


      The App asks for camera access for the AR ruler and height measure, and
      for location access ("While Using the App") for the compass's
      true-north heading. Motion sensors are read for the level, protractor,
      stud finder and metal detector. You can change the camera and location
      permissions at any time in iOS Settings.


      ## Your rights


      Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct,
      delete or port personal information, or to object to its processing.
      Because the App sends us no personal information and there is no
      account, the practical answer to almost every such request is deleting
      the App. If you believe we hold something about you, write to us at the
      address below and we will respond.


      ## Children


      The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
      collect personal information from children. Because there is no account
      and the App sends us nothing, we have no children's records to hold.


      ## Security


      The design is the security measure: no account to compromise, no server
      of ours receiving your measurements, and sensor and camera processing
      that never leaves the device.


      ## Changes to this policy


      We may update this policy as the App changes. The "last updated" date at
      the top of this page always reflects the current version, and material
      changes will be reflected in the App's disclosures in the same release.
