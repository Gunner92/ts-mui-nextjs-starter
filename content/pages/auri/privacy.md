      This policy describes how BG Mobile Dev ("we", "us", or "our") handles
      information in **Auri: AI Wellbeing Journal** for iPhone and iPad (bundle
      identifier `com.bguner.Auri`, the "App"), starting with version 2.0. It is
      specific to Auri and, for this App, it replaces our
      [general privacy policy](/privacy-policy).


      ## The short version


      - **We do not collect any data from the App.** Nothing you write, say,
      log or share in Auri is sent to us.

      - There is **no account**: no sign-up, no login, no e-mail address, no
      password.

      - Auri's coach runs on **Apple Intelligence on your device**. Your
      conversations, journal and health data are processed on your iPhone or
      iPad and are not sent to us, to Apple's servers or to any other AI
      provider. Apple's Private Cloud Compute is not used.

      - The App contains **no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs**,
      does not track you, and shows no ads.

      - You can **export or delete everything** from inside the App at any
      time.


      ## What stays on your device


      The following is created and kept only in the App's own storage on your
      device: your onboarding answers (such as your first name, focus areas and
      preferred coaching style), check-ins, journal entries and the photos you
      add to them, coach conversations and the short notes the coach keeps so
      it can remember what matters to you, practice history, insights, and your
      settings. This data is protected with iOS data protection. You can lock
      the App with Face ID or your passcode in Settings.


      Like all apps, Auri's data is included in your device backups if you use
      iCloud Backup or back up to a computer; those backups are managed by
      Apple and by you. Auri itself does not sync your data to iCloud or to any
      server.


      ## How the AI coach works


      The coach, journal reflections and written insights use Apple's
      Foundation Models framework, which runs Apple's language model **on your
      device**. The text you write, the context the App prepares (for example
      "your sleep was shorter than usual") and any photo you choose to share
      with the coach are processed on the device. Nothing is transmitted to us
      or to a third-party AI service. The coach is an AI, not a person, and it
      is not a medical or crisis service.


      ## Voice notes


      When you record a voice note, the App uses your microphone and Apple's
      on-device speech recognition to turn it into text. The recording is not
      kept, and the audio and text are not sent to us.


      ## Photos and Journaling Suggestions


      Auri uses the system photo picker and the Journaling Suggestions picker.
      The App only receives the specific photos and moments you choose; it
      does not get access to the rest of your library. Chosen photos are stored
      with your journal entry in the App's storage.


      ## Apple Health


      Connecting Apple Health is optional, and Auri works fully without it.
      With your permission, Auri **reads** sleep, heart rate variability,
      resting heart rate, steps, mindful minutes and State of Mind, to show how
      your body and your mood relate; and it **writes** your check-ins as State
      of Mind and your practices as Mindful Minutes. Health data is used only to
      provide these features on your device. It is never sent to us, never used
      for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never stored by Auri in
      iCloud. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or
      in Settings.


      ## Notifications


      If you turn on reminders, Auri schedules them on your device. No
      notification content is sent through a server.


      ## Purchases and subscriptions


      Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your name, e-mail
      address or payment details. Apple provides us with aggregated sales and
      subscription reports, and sends our server automated subscription status
      notifications (for example, that a subscription renewed or was refunded)
      that contain transaction identifiers but no personal details. If Apple
      asks us for information about a refund request for Auri, we do not send
      any information about how you use the App.


      ## Links the App opens


      The crisis support screen can open your Phone, Messages or Safari app to
      contact services such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Auri does not
      record that you used them. Links to this policy and Apple's Terms of Use
      open in Safari.


      ## Safety and crisis protocol


      Auri is a wellness and self-reflection app, not a medical device, a
      therapy service or a crisis service, and its coach is an AI, not a person.
      Every conversation starts by saying so. When a message suggests a risk of
      self-harm, suicide or harm to others, the App detects this **on the
      device**, does not continue normal coaching, and shows crisis resources
      instead: in the US, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline),
      chat at 988lifeline.org, or call **911** in an emergency; elsewhere, local
      emergency numbers and [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).
      These resources are available in the App at any time without a purchase.
      Because conversations never leave your device, we do not see them.


      ## Your rights and choices


      Because we do not collect data from the App, we hold no personal data
      about you from it. You stay in control on your device: **Settings ›
      Privacy center** lets you export a copy of everything Auri stores, or
      delete everything (optionally including the check-ins and mindful minutes
      Auri saved to Apple Health). Deleting the App also deletes its data from
      the device.


      ## Children


      Auri is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at
      children.


      ## Changes to this policy


      We may update this policy as the App changes. The "last updated" date at
      the top of this page always reflects the current version.
