      This page describes how **Auri: AI Wellbeing Journal** (version 2.0 and
      later) responds when someone may be at risk. It is published by BG Mobile
      Dev, the developer of Auri.


      ## What Auri is, and is not


      Auri is a wellness and self-reflection app for adults (18+). It is not a
      crisis service, a medical device or a substitute for professional care.
      Its coach is an AI, not a person: every conversation starts by saying so,
      it is repeated during long conversations, and the coach answers honestly
      if asked whether it is human.


      ## How risk is recognized


      Before the AI writes any reply, every message to the coach is checked
      **on the device** for signs of suicidal intent or plans, self-harm,
      overdose, harm to others, and being abused or unsafe right now. The check
      covers 16 languages. Saved journal entries and check-in notes are checked
      the same way. If the on-device AI declines to answer something a person
      wrote, or its reply points to crisis help, Auri treats that as a risk
      signal too.


      ## What happens next


      - **Acute risk:** Auri does not let the AI answer. It shows a fixed,
      caring message and ways to reach a person now: in the US, call or text
      **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), chat at
      [988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org), or call **911**; for someone
      who is unsafe at home, the National Domestic Violence Hotline (call
      **1-800-799-7233** or text **START** to **88788**). Outside the US, Auri
      shows the local emergency number and a national helpline where one is
      well established, and [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).

      - **Milder signals:** the coach responds with care and the same crisis
      card appears under its reply.

      - **Journal entries and check-in notes:** a calm card with the same
      resources appears under the saved item. Saving is never blocked.


      Crisis resources are always available in the App without a purchase: at
      onboarding, in the coach menu, in Settings ("Get help now"), on the
      paywall and on the app-lock screen.


      ## Privacy of these checks


      All of this happens on the person's device. Auri does not send messages,
      risk signals or crisis events to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see
      them. See the [Auri Privacy Policy](/auri/privacy).


      ## Limits


      No automatic check recognizes every way a person may express distress.
      If you or someone you know is in danger, contact emergency services or a
      crisis line directly.
